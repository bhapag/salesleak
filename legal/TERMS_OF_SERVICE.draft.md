## 2. What the Service does

SalesLeak is a sales follow-up and quotation tracker for businesses. It lets
your team record enquiries ("leads"), the customers they come from, quotations
issued against them, and follow-up tasks — and surfaces which of those appear
to need attention, including an estimated value of opportunities at risk.

Those risk figures, priorities and "money at risk" totals are **calculated
indicators derived from the data your team enters**. They are not advice, not a
prediction, and not a guarantee of any commercial outcome. You remain
responsible for your own sales decisions.
